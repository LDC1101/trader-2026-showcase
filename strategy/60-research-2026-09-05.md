Carried out according to `50-research-plan.md`. Every number is measured or backed by a
source; the scripts in `src/` reproduce them.

## Summary

1. **As the carrier of a 1% position, gold is twice as good as Brent**, both historically
   (one side survives 40 days: 32.8% vs 15.1%; one side reaches ≥10x: 19.1% vs 8.2%) and
   in practice (SG offers a gapless 1% ladder in both directions on gold at a 0.2% spread;
   on Brent, on Saturday, there is **no call closer than 3.0%**).
2. **Historically the winning mark is almost never reached.** +66% in 40 days occurred in
   0.82% of Brent windows since 1987 and never for gold in three years (best window 30x).
   Only names with 5% daily volatility get above 1% (SMR 3.5%, OKLO 1.4%), and SG has no
   usable products on them.
3. **Brent long is the only main-prize path with a live catalyst**, but only with a barrier
   at most 1.5% away. That exists only once SG issues new calls on Monday morning (SG issues
   near the money every day; last issue 2–3 Sep).
4. **The oil trend rule from three years of data was wrong.** On 2024–2026 it looked as if
   oil reversed after every trend (entering with the trend 1.7% survival, against it 14%).
   Over 39 years: 15.2% vs 15.1%. Dropped.
5. **Prediction markets instead of a model:** Hormuz traffic normal by 31 Oct **9.5%**,
   Iran-Oman agreement by 31 Oct **28%**, end of the US blockade by 31 Oct **35.5%**, Fed
   hike on 16 Sep **49.5%** ($98M volume).

## 6. Variants for Monday

**A: order card as planned.** Brent long + Brent short, 1–2%.
Data: one side alive 15.1%, ≥10x 8.2%, winning mark 0.14% (3 years) or 0.82% (long, 39
years). The catalyst is live and binary: a de-escalation event by 31 Oct is priced at
28–35% (−17 to −25% → short 9–13x), escalation is under way (+25–35% → long 26–36x;
$150 → 57x). **The only path to the winning mark that has a catalyst.**
Catch: on Saturday there is no call under 3%; if no call at 1.5% or closer is available on
Monday, Depot A is not a main-prize position.

**B: calculator.** Gold long + gold short, about 1.1% each. One side alive 32.8%, ≥10x
19.1%, winning mark 0%. Dated catalyst: the Fed on 16 Sep at 20:00 (during trading hours,
49.5%), plus managed money at a near-record short (−233k). Best path to a good rank and to
the weekly ranking, no path to the winning mark at 1%.

**C: mixed.** Depot A Brent long (winning-mark path), Depot B gold. As a complement to
Brent long, gold **short** is the counter-position (escalation → oil up → rate fears →
gold down; de-escalation the reverse). This gives up the measured opposite pair on a single
underlying; the pair statistics for gold short are weak on three years of bull market.

**Recommendation:** **A, with one condition and one rotation.**

Condition: Depot A only with a fresh Brent call at 1.5% or closer; in our experience SG
issues one on Monday morning. If none is available, **gold short FG6QX8** replaces the
escalation leg (escalation → oil up → rate fears → gold down; that is how August 2026
went), not the 3% call. The pair statistics point the other way here: Brent short + gold
short over three years 13.4% / 6.1% (≥10x), Brent short + gold long 34.2% / 20.3%. The
second value is the 2023–2026 gold bull market in the rear-view mirror, the first is the
scenario logic without a tailwind. Neither is a directional forecast. The fallback is a
judgment call, which is why the fresh Brent call comes first and the fallback second.

Rotation: after the first knock-out the affected depot moves to **gold**, opposite to the
other depot. That keeps the winning-mark path open, and the losing side runs on the
underlying with twice the survival rate.

**The player's decision, 6 Sep 2026: variant A. Rotation to gold only after the first
knock-out, no early rotation ahead of the Fed.**
