# Trader 2026 game rules: reference paraphrase

**This is an English paraphrase, not a translation.** The binding text was Société Générale's
German original on the game platform. The player copied sections 4–9 from the platform on 7 Sep
2026 (the platform was off limits to routines and sessions). Sections 3 and 10 are reconstructed
from the passages quoted in the pre-game notes; section 8 was not recorded. The section numbering
follows SG's text so that other files can cite "section 5". Conclusions are in `10-rules.md`.

From 8 Sep, every order recommendation started by re-reading section 5, at the player's
instruction: "You always have to read the rules first, before you tell me anything."

## What follows for the cash base (7 Sep 2026)

- **Discount, index and bonus certificates and ETC/ETN are explicitly tradable.**
- **"Leveraged derivatives" means warrants, turbo warrants and factor warrants** (the definition
  sits in the 20,000-unit rule). Discount and index certificates do not fall under it: only the
  20%-per-security rule applies to them, not the €20,000 cap.
- **Matured products are removed and credited within 2 bank working days.** A discount certificate
  maturing on 23 Oct is cash by 27 Oct at the latest.
- **Dividends do not appear in the rules.** Corporate actions are entered manually and possibly
  late, with a reserved right to cash settlement. Ex-dividend dates inside the game window are a
  risk without documented compensation: avoid stocks with an ex-date up to 30 Oct, or price in
  the markdown.
- **A rule breach at execution reduces the order instead of rejecting it** ("the order is reduced
  accordingly"). This applies to orders that execute later. The immediate rejection on 7 Sep
  (4,040 × €4.95 = €19,998.00, plus the €3.90 fee) was the check at order entry.
- **Weekly ranking:** start value = end value of the previous week; after a reset €100,000. In
  addition there is a prize for the best **daily performance** over the full eight weeks and a
  weekly special prize among all active participants.
- **Valuation** at the last bid, at least €0.001.
- Orders can be cancelled only after the first order check.

## 3. Reset

If the game does not develop the way a player had hoped, each depot can be reset once per week.
A reset returns the depot to €100,000, cannot be undone and deletes all of the depot's game data.
For the weekly ranking a depot reset during the week starts at €100,000 (section 9).

## 4. Tradable securities

**Shares.** All shares listed on the Stuttgart exchange with a traded price of at least €1.00 at
the time of purchase.

**Derivatives.** In addition, all derivatives issued by Société Générale and listed on EUWAX in
Stuttgart, for example index certificates, bonus certificates, discount certificates, ETC/ETN,
warrants, turbo warrants, factor warrants and inline warrants. New issues become tradable no later
than one day after issue. Not tradable: any derivative that accrues interest, such as reverse
convertibles.

## 5. Trading

**20% rule and €20,000 rule.** Securities are bought and sold with the "Order" button. In total, at
most 20% of the depot value may be spent on any one security. Likewise, at most 20% of the depot
value may be invested in leveraged derivatives. On top of that, leveraged derivatives are capped
at €20,000 regardless of the depot value. Both rules apply at the time of purchase and are
computed on the ask of the product being bought, for shares and derivatives alike.

**20,000-unit rule.** To keep conditions realistic, leveraged derivatives (warrants, turbo warrants
and factor warrants) are also capped at 20,000 units. If an order cannot execute immediately, for
example because of a limit or the holding period, and is no longer compliant at the time of
execution, it is reduced accordingly.

**Buy orders per day.** At most 20 buy orders per depot per game day. Sales are unlimited.

**Holding period.** Every purchase carries a 5-minute holding period; the position can be sold only
after it ends. Sell orders, limit orders included, can be placed right after the purchase, but they
activate only after 5 minutes and then execute at current prices.

**Execution.** Orders execute against real-time prices. For shares and derivatives listed on EUWAX,
an unlimited order executes immediately (subject to the holding period) at the current bid/ask.

**Order types.**

| Type | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| unlimited (buy at market / sell at market) | executes immediately at the current bid/ask |
| limit (buy or sell) | executes once the bid/ask reaches the limit: at least the limit for a sale, at most the limit for a purchase |
| stop-buy | becomes a market buy once the stop-buy price is reached |
| stop-loss | becomes a market sell once the stop-loss price is reached |
| stop-buy-limit | becomes a limit buy once the stop-buy-limit price is reached |
| stop-loss-limit | becomes a limit sell once the stop-loss-limit price is reached |

**Validity.** Orders are either day orders or valid for 7 days. Day orders are deleted at midnight;
7-day orders expire 7 calendar days after they were placed.

**Trading hours.** Börse Stuttgart shares 8:00–22:00, EUWAX derivatives 8:00–22:00. No off-exchange
trading. Only prices that arise during the current day's trading hours are used for execution.

**Cancellation.** The order book shows all orders not yet executed. Buy and sell orders can be
cancelled only after the first order check.

**Irregularities.** The game management may correct or cancel executions after the fact.

## 6. Transaction costs

Shares: €10.00 per execution. Derivatives: flat fee of €3.90 per execution.

## 7. Depot

**Corporate actions** are generally taken into account, but may be booked late because the
Trader 2026 team enters them by hand. Depending on the type, the team may settle them in cash.

**Valuation.** To determine the weekly winner, every security is valued at no less than €0.001 in
both the starting and the final depot value. If no last bid is available, the last traded price is
used.

**Maturities and knock-outs.** Matured or knocked-out products are removed within at most 2 bank
working days and their value is credited to cash.

## 9. Scoring

Rankings are calculated and updated overnight. They are based on the absolute change in the
depots' total value over the period: the current values of the positions plus cash. Gains do not
have to be realised.

A weekly win goes to the highest percentage gain over the past game week, determined in the night
from Sunday to Monday with a final check. A new game week starts on Monday. The week's starting
value is the previous week's end value; for depots created or reset during the week, €100,000 is
the starting point.

At the end of the game, the participant with the best daily performance is also awarded (percentage
change between the previous day's close and the scoring day's close, best over the whole game).
Each week there is also a special prize among all active participants.

The overall winner is the player whose depot holds the highest absolute value at the end of the
last game day. Every player has a weekly rank and an overall rank; both depots are considered in
the ranking.

A prize for the best private game group (at least five active players) goes to the group with the
highest average percentage performance of its depots from the starting value to the final value on
the last game day; it is paid to the group's creator.

## 10. Fair play (Rule 10)

Prohibited: manipulative behaviour, multiple user names, and unauthorised aids (for example
automated trading systems). Named explicitly as manipulative: shrinking the depot volume through a
series of trades in order to lift a single position's share above the 20% limit.

A violation or a justified suspicion leads to exclusion without a hearing; running and already
awarded winnings are forfeited. A second user name leads to immediate exclusion, and both depots
drop out of the ranking.

## Other conditions (section not recorded)

The profile must hold the player's full name and address before the first order. Placing the
first order with an incomplete profile forfeits any prize claim permanently.
