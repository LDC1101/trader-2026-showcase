# Hormuz dossier, as of 6 Sep 2026

Memory for the assessment. Sources: Wikipedia "2026 Strait of Hormuz campaign" and "2026
Iran war ceasefire", Al Jazeera 26 Aug, CNN 8 Aug, PBS/NBC 19 Aug, Forbes 1 Sep, EIA daily
closes for all prices. Kept up to date as events continue.

## Summary

- War since **28 Feb 2026** (US-Israeli air strikes, Ali Khamenei killed). Iran closes
  Hormuz the same day. New Supreme Leader since 8 Mar: **Mojtaba Khamenei**. President
  **Pezeshkian**, foreign minister **Araghchi**, parliament speaker **Ghalibaf**.
- Brent before the war **$71**, high **$138 on 7 Apr**, low of the hope phase **$76 on
  22 Jun**, today **$96**.
- **There already was an agreement:** the Trump-Pezeshkian MoU of 17 Jun (Islamabad
  Memorandum, signed in Versailles), with a 60-day deadline. The strait opened on 18 Jun and
  the US blockade ended. Collapse on **8 Jul**, blockade again from **14/15 Jul**, deadline
  expired on **19 Aug** without extension. Each side accuses the other of breaking it.
- **Current negotiating track:** the Iran-Oman corridor (Araghchi/Albusaidi, Tehran,
  26 Aug): a joint temporary shipping corridor and mine clearance. According to Iran, the
  opening comes only with a US quid pro quo (end of the blockade, return to the June
  agreement, compensation). The US has not agreed; Trump threatened Oman a week earlier.
  Qatar and Pakistan are mediating direct talks.
- **Military situation:** tankers are hit almost daily (UKMTO 107–127, Khasab, Limah,
  Kumzar). The US destroyed an Iranian tanker on 4/5 Sep and damaged two. More than 100
  mines cleared from the traffic separation scheme (25 Aug). 6 instead of 85 ships a day.

## Implications for the assessment

1. **"Agreement reported" means signed and implemented**: strait open, blockade lifted,
   transit confirmed. A report of being "close to a deal" (5 Aug) or a partial agreement
   without the US (26 Aug) did not turn the price. Only the MoU did, and even that by just
   6%.
2. **The move runs ahead of the report.** Between 6 May and 22 Jun Brent fell 45%; the
   agreement came at the end of that stretch. Closing Depot A only when the agreement is
   reported means having already suffered the whole way there. For that reason the
   Polymarket probability matters more as an *early indicator* than the headline: it moves
   during the hope phase, the headline at the end.
3. **Today differs from June.** Back then the opening was expected (price 80 after 138).
   Today Brent is at 96 and Polymarket gives an opening by 31 Oct 9.5%. A real opening now
   would be a surprise and would move the price by correspondingly more than 6%, but not as
   a jump on the day of signing: over the days in which it becomes apparent.
4. **Escalation pays only with a supply outage.** 8 Jul brought +47% because the strait,
   just reopened, closed again. Another tanker hit while the strait is already closed
   changes nothing about supply; the comparison cases (Abqaiq, Soleimani, the 2019 tanker
   attacks) all fell back within 20 days. Size "large" is earned only by what changes the
   flow itself: Kharg Island infrastructure, mines in the corridor, Saudi or Emirati
   facilities, Bab el-Mandeb.
5. **Ceasefires here hold briefly.** The only one so far held 21 days. A reported
   understanding is an event with a half-life, not an endpoint.
