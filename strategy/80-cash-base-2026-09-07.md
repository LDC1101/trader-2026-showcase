# The €80,000 of cash per depot: research, 7 Sep 2026

Question (the player): are there stocks that will deliver safe, if small, gains over the
next 8 weeks, and should the cash sit there? Answer in three parts: what is measured, what
the game offers, what follows.

## 1. What "safe" means over 8 weeks: measured

40-trading-day windows, closing prices, 9/2023–9/2026 (repo data). "Crisis" = since
28 Feb 2026; that is 91 heavily overlapping windows, so only a few independent
observations.

| Series | Median | P(>0) | P(>+2%) | P(<−5%) | 5% quantile | 95% quantile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S&P 500 | +3.6% | 80% | 64% | 6% | −5.5% | +11.0% |
| Nasdaq-100 | +4.3% | 73% | 62% | 9% | −7.5% | +16.3% |
| DAX | +2.9% | 76% | 59% | 5% | −5.0% | +11.3% |
| TLT (US Treasuries) | −1.0% | 40% | 27% | 12% | −6.5% | +7.5% |
| GE Vernova | +14.7% | 77% | 73% | 15% | −13.7% | +50.3% |
| Nvidia | +5.8% | 70% | 62% | 18% | −15.8% | +48.8% |

No stock and no index is "safe" over 8 weeks: the broadest index loses in one window in
five, and loses more than 5% in one in twenty. Single stocks with a high median (GE
Vernova) lose more than 5% in one window in seven.

**In the crisis, stocks move against oil.** 5-day correlation of Brent with the S&P −0.45,
with the DAX −0.59 (3 years: −0.03 / −0.20). On days with Brent above +3% the S&P fell
0.41% at the median; on days with Brent below −3% it rose 0.66%. Over 40 days, conditional
on Brent:

| Condition (40 days) | Period | n | S&P median | DAX median | Nasdaq median | S&P P(>0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brent < −10% (agreement) | 3 y | 127 | +2.7% | +3.2% | +4.1% | 70% |
| Brent > +10% (escalation) | 3 y | 106 | +2.6% | +2.5% | 0.0% | 68% |
| Brent < −10% | crisis | 35 | +3.6% | +3.6% | +7.0% | 100% |
| Brent > +10% | crisis | 23 | +3.7% | +3.9% | −0.3% | 100% |

Consequence: a stock basket fits the winning branch of **Depot B** (agreement → oil falls,
stocks rise). For Depot A (escalation) the Nasdaq is the wrong basket; S&P and DAX are
neutral.

## 2. What the game offers

Tradable: every stock listed in Stuttgart at €1 or more, and every SG derivative without
accrued interest (bonds and reverse convertibles are excluded). Matured products are booked
out within two banking days and credited to cash. 20% per security; the €20,000 limit
applies only to **leveraged** derivatives. Investment products (discount and index
certificates) are not covered by it (check in the order form: "available for leveraged
products" must not change).

### b) Discount certificates: the "safe small gain"

SG discount certificates maturing on **23 Oct 2026** (inside the game window, credited by
27 Oct) with the cap below the current price. At maturity you receive the cap if the index
stands above it. As of 7 Sep 10:15, spot DAX 25,942, Euro Stoxx 6,373:

| WKN | Underlying | Cap | Buffer | Ask | Value at maturity | Gain to 2026-10-23 | p.a. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FD0ARM** | DAX | 24,900 | 4.0% | 246.71 | 249.00 | **+0.93%** | 6.7% |
| **FD0ARL** | DAX | 24,850 | 4.2% | 246.28 | 248.50 | +0.90% | 6.5% |
| **FD0ARK** | DAX | 24,800 | 4.4% | 245.86 | 248.00 | +0.87% | 6.3% |
| **FD6RLH** | Euro Stoxx 50 | 6,100 | 4.3% | 60.46 | 61.00 | +0.89% | 6.7% |
| FE1ZZU | Euro Stoxx 50 | 6,050 | 5.1% | 60.02 | 60.50 | +0.80% | 6.0% |

Four different WKNs at 20% each = €80,000, spread 0.0–0.1%, €3.90 per order. Return about
**+€700 per depot** by 27 Oct. A loss only if the DAX or the Euro Stoxx stands more than 4%
below today's level on 23 Oct; the loss is then the index loss minus the buffer. Measured:
DAX below −5% over 40 days in 5.0% of windows (3 years), in the crisis 0 of 92. Until
maturity the price moves with the index (delta about 0.3–0.4); a DAX drop of 5% in between
costs about 1.5% temporarily.

With a 14% buffer (FA12Q8, maturing 25 Sep) the yield falls to 1.9% p.a., the money-market
rate: 0.1% in 18 days.

Discount certificates maturing after 30 Oct (27 Nov, 28 Dec) yield 7–8% p.a. but have to be
sold on 29 Oct, when their price still depends on the index. No advantage.

### c) Index certificates / stock basket: higher expectation, real risk

SG index certificates without maturity (class 27): DAX e.g. CJ8M7K, Euro Stoxx CJ8V3P, MDAX
CJ8V3G; S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 are available (USD products without quanto carry EUR/USD
risk, ±3% over 8 weeks). Expectation as in table 1: median +3 to +4%, P(>0) about 80%, 5%
quantile −5%. For Depot B in its winning branch (agreement) measured +3.6% for the S&P and
+7% for the Nasdaq. Dividends: how the game treats them is not documented. Index
certificates on price indices (S&P, Nasdaq, Euro Stoxx) lose the dividend (about 0.3% over
8 weeks); the DAX is a performance index.

## 3. What follows

The main prize depends on the leveraged position, and no basket changes that: the base
delivers +€700 (discount) to +€3,000 (index) on a depot that has to reach €1.35M for first
place. The weekly prize goes to a depot with +100%, which the base cannot reach either.
What the base does move is the **rank** in the many weeks in which the depot sits at
€80,000 plus the base without a live leveraged position, and the depot value if a position
runs to the end.

A **reset** wipes the base as well. Cost per cycle: 4 × €3.90 (discount) or 4 × €3.90 (SG
index certificate) or 4 × €10 (stocks). At +0.9% return that is earned back after two
trading days, so there is no reason to wait.

**Recommendation:**

| | Depot A (Brent call, escalation) | Depot B (Brent put, agreement) |
|---|---|---|
| Base | 4 discounts maturing 2026-10-23: FD0ARM, FD0ARL, FD0ARK, FD6RLH | 4 discounts as in A **or** an index basket (DAX CJ8M7K, Euro Stoxx CJ8V3P, MDAX CJ8V3G + 1 discount) |
| Why | in the winning branch stocks tend to fall; the discount is capped and has the buffer | in the winning branch stocks rise (+3.6% measured); the index captures that |
| Expectation to 2026-10-30 | +€700 | +€700 (discount) / about +€2,500 (index, P(>0) 80%, 5% quantile −€4,000) |
| After a reset | buy again, €15.60 | buy again, €15.60 |

Units per discount certificate: (20,000 − 3.90) ÷ ask, rounded down; for FD0ARM 81 units =
€19,983.51. Buy with a limit at ask + €0.05, valid for the day. **No** sell order: maturity
on 23 Oct pays out automatically. After a reset, buy everything again.

Addendum 7 Sep 11:00, rules text supplied by the player (`11-rules-reference.md`): discount
certificates are explicitly tradable and do **not** count as leveraged derivatives (the rule
names warrants, turbo warrants and factor warrants); maturities are credited within 2
banking days. Dividends are not covered by the rules text, which is one more reason to hold
certificates instead of stocks during the game window.
