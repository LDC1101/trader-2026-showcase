# Sample briefings

Between 5 Sep and 10 Nov 2026, 59 briefings were written, mostly by the cloud routines: morning
briefings, evening checks, weekly reviews and a final review. 51 of them date up to the final review
on 1 Nov; 8 more followed after the game ended, until the routines were paused. Each cloud briefing
was delivered as a repo commit, because Telegram is not reachable from the cloud. The three files here
are representative translated samples:

- [2026-10-21-morning-briefing.md](2026-10-21-morning-briefing.md): a morning briefing on a trade day
  (Brent at a five-month high, both portfolios in cash, no forced purchase), followed by the 09:10
  addendum from a session with the player that lists alternatives to oil for a day bet, with
  candidates, concrete orders and open unknowns. The player bought a Tesla BEST call that morning.
- [2026-09-18-abort-condition-check.md](2026-09-18-abort-condition-check.md): a morning briefing in
  which the abort condition "three knock-outs in a row, same instrument type: switch from turbo to
  warrant" fires, with the full abort-condition table and the evening check that re-checks Hormuz.
- [2026-10-30-evening-check.md](2026-10-30-evening-check.md): the evening check of the last game day,
  run through the five standard checks (Hormuz, knock-out, other abort conditions, expiring orders,
  reset), and an example of a routine working from a stale positions file.

The weekly reviews are translated in full under [../weekly/](../weekly/) and the final review in
[../final-review.md](../final-review.md). The full German originals of all briefings are in the
private archive.
