### What happened: decision and outcome kept apart

**Depot A (main-prize leg):** four trades, ends at +61 %.
- Started as a gold put (substitute, because on 7 Sep no Brent call with ≤1.5 % distance to
  barrier was available). Within an hour it was swapped at a small profit into the Brent BEST
  call FG6GK5 when SG listed new calls at 9:30.
- The call ran to **+305 %** by 10 Sep (€1.38 → €5.59), then was sold: proceeds €80,990, Depot A
  at €161,223 cash.
- **Decision:** the sale deliberately and with reasons broke the rule "never take profits". The
  turbo had used up its leverage (14,489 units, 6.1 % distance to barrier). A simulation (150k
  paths) showed that selling and re-entering tight raises P(>500k) from 6.9 % to 9.6 %. The rule
  "taking profits costs a factor of 93" measures a position that *keeps* its leverage, which was
  not the case here.
- **Outcome:** the planned tight re-entry **did not happen.** The buy limit (FG6H9E, €2.15) only
  filled on a pullback that never came; Brent jumped to 105 (+4 %) on 10 Sep. Depot A has been in
  cash since. **Held, Depot A would stand at ~€197,000 instead of €161,223.** Giving up ~€36,000
  of book profit is the cost of selling without a successful re-entry.
- **No lesson from a single trade:** whether sell-and-rebuy beats holding is decided by the
  simulation, not by three trades, and the simulation says "yes, if the re-entry succeeds". The
  process point for Monday: the re-entry has to **actually happen** and must not fail on a limit
  that is too tight (see MONDAY).

**Depot B (directional weekly bet):** three trades, ends at −18 %.
- Brent put FC8QQU (knocked out 8 Sep, Brent rose overnight, −€16,368). Reset to €100,000.
- Brent call FG6GLL (held briefly, +€1,352), then gold BEST call FG6PCD (€19,498), because

- Gold call FG6PCD **knocked out Friday 11 Sep** (gold fell below the 4,315.89 barrier before the

that the 20 % limit binds and caps the leverage earlier). The reset is available again from
